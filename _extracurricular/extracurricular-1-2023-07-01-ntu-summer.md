- Led the delegation in academic exchanges focusing on robotics, data mining, and advanced algorithms.
- Provided simultaneous translation for academic seminars, ensuring accurate and efficient cross-cultural communication.
- Served as the official student representative of the 39-member delegation; delivered speeches at the opening and closing ceremonies, sharing insights on undergraduate research in computer science and petroleum engineering.
